About

I am an eco-hydrological modeller working on agricultural nitrogen and water quality in the Indo-Gangetic Plain. My doctoral research uses coupled surface water and groundwater modelling to quantify how nitrogen applied to intensively irrigated cropland is redistributed among soil, crop, aquifer, and atmosphere over multi-decadal timescales, and to evaluate management practices that reduce groundwater nitrate concentrations without compromising crop yields.

The work is carried out within HIROS (Hindon Root Sensing: River Rejuvenation through Scalable Water and Solute Balance Modeling and Informed Farmers Action), an India–Netherlands collaboration under the Clean Ganga Mission, and has been presented at international meetings in Asia and Europe. My thesis is supervised by Dr. Idhayachandhiran Ilampooranan.

Research interests

  • Eco-hydrological and water quality modelling (SWAT+, MODFLOW)
  • Agricultural nitrogen cycling
  • Groundwater nitrate dynamics
  • Greenhouse gas emissions from cropland
  • Best management practice evaluation
  • Remote sensing for water resources
