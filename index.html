01 — About

About me

I am a PhD candidate and Senior Research Fellow in the Department of Water Resources Development and Management, Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, supervised by Dr. Idhayachandhiran Ilampooranan.

Decades of fertiliser application in the Indo-Gangetic Plain have moved large quantities of reactive nitrogen into soil organic matter, the unsaturated zone, the aquifer, and the atmosphere. Because subsurface transport is slow, groundwater nitrate concentrations observed today reflect management applied over preceding decades, and the response to any change in management is correspondingly delayed. My work builds a long-term nitrogen budget for the Hindon River Basin to resolve where applied nitrogen accumulates, and on what timescale it is released.

HIROS — Hindon Root Sensing: River Rejuvenation through Scalable Water and Solute Balance Modeling and Informed Farmers Action. An India–Netherlands collaborative project under the Clean Ganga Mission, with partners including Wageningen University & Research, TU Delft, and ICAR-CSSRI Karnal.

02 — Research

Research themes

Theme 01

Nitrogen redistribution

A long-term budget of nitrogen across soil, crop, groundwater, and atmospheric compartments, resolving accumulation and release timescales under rice–wheat and sugarcane rotations.

Theme 02

Coupled surface water and groundwater modelling

SWAT+ coupled to gwflow, so nitrate leached below the root zone is tracked through the aquifer rather than treated as a terminal loss.

Theme 03

Best management practice evaluation

A factorial set of tillage and nutrient management scenarios assessed on leaching, in-stream loads, soil organic carbon, greenhouse gas emissions, and yield.

Theme 04

Water quality data compilation

Long-term groundwater nitrate and stream water quality records from national monitoring archives, with censored-data handling and gap filling.
