Teaching & Seminars

Seminars presented, training received, and international research exposure.

Departmental seminars presented

  • Management control on soil organic carbon; analysis of factors controlling soil organic matter levels in Great Plains grasslands (CENTURY model).
  • Utilisation of satellite-based hydrometeorological products in watershed hydrology.
  • Rainfall–runoff modelling using ArcSWAT.
  • Flood inundation mapping.
  • Prediction of groundwater levels using machine learning.

International training and research exposure

  • 1st mHM Workshop, Helmholtz Centre for Environmental Research (UFZ), Leipzig, Germany, 3–7 November 2025. Selected participant; travel and accommodation grant awarded by the organisers. Training in the mesoscale hydrological model (mHM).
  • Research visit, Wageningen University & Research and TU Delft, Netherlands, 2–13 December 2024. Collaborative research stay under the HIROS project, including model intercomparison discussions and presentation of Hindon basin results to Dutch project partners.

Training and workshops attended

  • Hydrological modelling using R, Department of Water Resources Development and Management, IIT Roorkee.
  • Short-term course on groundwater hydrogeological investigations, National Institute of Technology Raipur, covering delineation of groundwater potential zones using electrical resistivity analysis.
